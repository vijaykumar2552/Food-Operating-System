# DBMS concepts demonstrated

- Normalized operational tables separate users, customer profiles, restaurants, menu data, orders and payments.
- Foreign keys prevent orphaned business records.
- Check constraints protect quantities, prices and ratings.
- Composite indexes support customer order history and restaurant status queries.
- Views provide reusable reporting surfaces without copying data.
- Functions encapsulate deterministic pricing rules.
- Procedures provide callable reporting and status operations.
- Triggers maintain order status history as an integrity-adjacent audit trail.
- InnoDB supports atomic transactions and row locking for the future checkout flow.
